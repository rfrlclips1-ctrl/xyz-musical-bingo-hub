XY&Z QUICK MANUAL HOST MODE
===========================

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Open /host.html
2. Open "Host key settings"
3. Paste the private HOST_BRIDGE_KEY
4. Click "Save on this device"

EACH EVENT
1. Choose the venue and round
2. Click GO LIVE
3. Copy/paste: Song Title — Artist
4. Press Enter
5. Repeat for each song
6. Click END SESSION when finished

Accepted paste formats:
Song Title — Artist
Song Title | Artist
Song Title - Artist
Song Title on line 1, Artist on line 2

No Spotify login, djay integration, desktop bridge, or extra software is required.
